About me

Engineering with
purpose

I am Tiago Vilas, a Mechanical Engineering student at the Universite de Technologie de Troyes (UTT), graduating in 2029.

My path has taken me from France to Canada and back — working on projects that range from intelligent prosthetic hands to sport engineering ergometers and defense innovation. I thrive at the intersection of mechanics, biomechanics, and human-centered design.

As co-founder of A2M, I lead both the technical development and project management of a low-cost, adaptive prosthetic hand — because I believe technology should serve people, not the other way around.

Beyond engineering, I am driven by high performance: I practice judo and rugby, and I am currently engaged in the Concours Innovation COS, an inter-school defense engineering competition.

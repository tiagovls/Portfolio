TNEV —
Target-Tracking Robot

An Arduino-powered mechatronics robot that follows a line autonomously and launches a cork precisely into a moving bin from 3 meters.

Robot line-following demo — parcours autonome

Overview

The TNEV course at UTT is a full mechatronics project course: from design to prototyping to embedded programming. Our team designed and built a robot capable of following a line on the ground autonomously and, at the right moment, launching a cork into a moving bin from a distance of 3 meters.

The project covered the complete engineering cycle: mechanical design, 3D printing, electronic wiring, and embedded programming in C++ on an Arduino platform.

Cork-launching demo — tir sur cible mobile

Technical Breakdown

Mechanical structure — Designed in CAD and printed in FDM 3D printing. The chassis integrates motor mounts, sensor holders, and the cork launcher mechanism.

Embedded code (C++) — Written entirely in Arduino C++. PID control loop for smooth and precise line-following. Targeting algorithm computes timing and angle for the cork-launching actuator based on sensor input.

Electronics — IR sensors for line detection, servo motors for the launcher, DC motors for drive. Power management and wiring handled entirely by the team.

Project management — Full team coordination: task allocation, timeline, budget control, and final demonstration under competition conditions.

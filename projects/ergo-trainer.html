Ergo Trainer
Kayak Ergometer

An innovative kayak dry-land training machine featuring an adjustable pendulum system — designed, engineered, and manufactured from scratch for elite athletes.

ClientAQUA INNOV

RoleMechanical Design & Manufacturing

What is an Ergometer?

Kayak ergometers already exist — they allow athletes to train on dry land, replicating the paddling motion without being on water. However, conventional ergometers all share the same fundamental flaw: they are completely static and rigid. The machine stays perfectly still.

Yet anyone who has paddled a kayak knows: stability is the hardest part. The boat rocks, tilts, and responds to every movement of the paddler. Maintaining balance while generating maximum power is the core challenge of elite kayaking — and existing ergometers simply ignore it.

Our Innovation — The Adjustable Pendulum System

Our team's key innovation was the design of a fully original adjustable pendulum system integrated into the ergometer seat platform. Rather than sitting on a fixed frame, the athlete sits on a seat that can tilt laterally — mimicking the rocking motion of a kayak on water.

The pendulum's range of motion and resistance are adjustable, allowing coaches to calibrate the difficulty level: from near-static for beginners, to high-amplitude oscillation replicating choppy conditions for elite athletes.

This is the first ergometer specifically engineered to train balance and core stabilization in addition to paddling power — making it a completely novel product on the market.

My Role — End-to-End Engineering

This was a full-scope engineering project and I handled every stage of the mechanical work:

Research — Analysis of existing ergometer solutions on the market, biomechanical study of kayak paddling, identification of the core stability gap that all existing products miss.

Conception & CAD Design — Full 3D modeling of the ergometer structure, seat, and pendulum mechanism. Dimensioning and material selection for the structural components.

Workshop Manufacturing — Hands-on fabrication: turning, milling, and welding to build the prototype. Every metal component was machined or assembled by the mechanical team.

Our team was structured into two complementary groups: the technical team (mechanical engineers responsible for research, 3D CAD design, and full workshop fabrication) and the commercial & pitch team. Together, we achieved a major milestone — our team won a prestigious local laureate award at a speech/pitch competition, defending our innovation in front of a jury of entrepreneurs and professional engineers.

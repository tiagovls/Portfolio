Overview

A full parametric 3D model of an electric kettle engineered in PTC CREO Parametric. The design is built with strict geometric associativity: the entire CAD model is fully parameterized so that if you alter any single dimension, the entire assembly updates together seamlessly.

Each of the 4 main components was modeled separately, assembled with kinematic and positioning constraints, and driven by a master parameter table — ensuring wall thicknesses, mating surfaces, and handle radii adapt automatically whenever global dimensions are adjusted.

Technical Highlights

Master Parameter Table & Full Associativity — Height, base diameter, wall thickness, and handle radius are all driven from a central parameter sheet. Changing one dimension automatically cascades through all 4 parts and assembly interfaces.

4 Interlocking Components — Bowl (body), ergonomic handle, lid mechanism, and electrical power base — each modeled individually with dynamic parametric links.

Assembly Constraints — Complete assembly defined by coincidence, concentricity, and mate constraints that maintain stability under all parametric permutations.

Technical Drawing (Mise en plan) — Standardized A3 technical drawing including full dimensioning, section views, detail views, and tolerance annotations.

Rendering & Exploded Views — Material definitions (high-gloss polymer, stainless steel, glass level gauge) and exploded assembly perspectives.

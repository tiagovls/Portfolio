Overview

The objective was to redesign the iconic Pixar desk lamp from scratch using CATIA V5, demonstrating mastery of advanced surface modeling tools, boolean geometry operations, in-context assembly design, and robust parametric modelling.

The entire CAD model was engineered to be fully parametric: all sketches, reference planes, equations, and assembly constraints are dynamically linked. If any single dimension is modified, the entire lamp geometry updates cohesively — with the articulating arm segments, lampshade proportions, and in-context springs dynamically recalculating without rebuild errors or broken references.

The goal was not a simple copy but a creative reimagination of the lamp — introducing original geometry in the lampshade and base while preserving the articulated, poseable character of the original.

Technical Highlights

Fully Parametric Model Architecture — Built with strict parametric relations: modifying any single driving dimension or coordinate automatically shifts and updates the entire model, ensuring robust design intent throughout.

Generative Shape Design (GSD) — Lampshade designed using CATIA surface modeling workshops. Multi-section surfaces, fillets, and trim operations for a clean, organic form.

Boolean Operations — Base modeled using union and subtract operations to create creative geometric cutouts and interlocking volumes.

In-Context Springs — Springs designed in assembly context — their geometry adapts dynamically as the lamp arms are repositioned, maintaining geometric coherence at any posture.

Full Assembly — All parts assembled with kinematic constraints allowing articulation. Multiple posture configurations tested and rendered.

Bulb & Cable Integration — Electrical cable routed through the arm with proper socket interface at the lampshade.

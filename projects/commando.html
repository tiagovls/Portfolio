What is the Concours Innovation COS?

The Concours Innovation COS is a personal challenge — a defense engineering innovation competition in which teams of engineering students from various French schools work to design solutions for real operational problems submitted by the French armed forces.

Unlike large public competitions, this challenge is organized on a school-by-school basis: each institution can field one or several teams, with each team tackling a military challenge independently. There is no standard ranking between all schools — it is a serious professional exercise, treated as a real engineering mission.

The challenges come directly from military personnel who describe genuine unmet needs on the battlefield. The problems are operational, current, and technically demanding.

The Challenge: Counter-Drone Technology

Drones have become the dominant tactical vector of modern warfare. Cheap, fast, autonomous, and increasingly intelligent — they represent a threat that conventional defense systems were not built to handle effectively.

The military needs expressed through this competition include detection, identification, jamming, interception, and physical neutralization of drones — at various scales, from commercial off-the-shelf threats to more sophisticated adversarial drones.

Our team is currently in the early subject-selection phase. The exact technical direction will be updated here as the project progresses.

Why Participate?

This competition pushes me into a context I find genuinely motivating — engineering with direct, real-world stakes. Beyond academic exercises, this is about:

— Working on a problem that matters, defined by people whose lives depend on the solutions

— Developing cross-disciplinary thinking: electronics, mechanics, software, system integration, human factors

— Learning to communicate with military stakeholders — translating engineering concepts for non-technical decision-makers

— Testing my ability to work under pressure, with incomplete information and evolving constraints
